title: Inbound webhooks
description: Route selected IMAP mailboxes to signed HTTPS endpoints.

## Access

Inbound accounts and routes contain organization-wide mail. Only owners and administrators can view or change them.

## Create a route

1. Add an IMAP account and test the connection.
2. Add one or more mailbox folders.
3. Create an HTTPS webhook with a random secret of at least 32 characters.
4. Select only the mailboxes the destination needs.
5. Add sender filters when the consumer handles a narrow source set.

Private, loopback, link-local, and metadata-network destinations are blocked unless the deployment explicitly enables private webhook networks.

## Verify signatures

Validate the timestamp and HMAC signature before parsing or persisting a request. Reject stale timestamps and compare signatures with a timing-safe function. Keep the webhook secret in a secret manager.

Use **Send test** to inspect the exact headers and body expected by your consumer. Do not log `rawMimeBase64` in production.

## Delivery failures

Webhook delivery retries with backoff. A successful HTTP status should be returned only after the consumer has durably accepted the event.
